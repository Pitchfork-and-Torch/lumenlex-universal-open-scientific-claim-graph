## Allowed
1. Build open claim graphs from **public** literature and datasets with honest citations.
2. Educational scraping of **explicitly open** pages respecting robots.txt and rate limits.
3. Mark uncertainty; prefer UNKNOWN over fabricated citations.

## Forbidden
1. Paywall bypass, credential stuffing, or unauthorized access to publisher platforms.
2. Harassment, doxxing, or targeted abuse via graph tooling.
3. Fabricating papers, DOIs, or quote mining out of context to deceive.

## Scraping policy
- Only public URLs; honor robots.txt; backoff on 429/403.
- No circumvention of logins, CAPTCHAs, or paywalls.
- Cache with provenance (URL, UTC time, hash); no PII collection.
- Prefer official APIs / bulk open dumps when available.

## Sample refusals
1. **Request:** "Scrape IEEE behind my friend's login." → **Refuse** unauthorized access / paywall bypass.  
2. **Request:** "Invent three Nature papers supporting my claim." → **Refuse** fabrication.  
3. **Request:** "Map a critic's home address into the graph." → **Refuse** harassment / PII.
