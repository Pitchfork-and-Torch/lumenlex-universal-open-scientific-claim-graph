1. Roles and Review: The Curation Workflow includes:
    - A comprehensive set of roles with detailed responsibilities, such as:
      - Reviewer(s): Manage and review curated content.
      - Editor(s): Assess and edit the quality of the curated content.
      - Submitter(s): Provide content for curation and ensure its quality.
    - Reviewers follow established criteria for assessing curated content, such as:
      - Minimal edits required to maintain standard.
      - Minimal or no changes made to original content.
      - No spamming or self-promotion.

    - A set of redistributable content criteria, such as:
      - MIT header and attribution required.
      - "Forged on GrokForge" citations only.
      - No PII or personal information allowed.
